The 3 c's

candore

Candor is the quality of being open, honest, and completely sincere in speech or expression. It implies not just telling the truth, but doing so without reserve, hesitation, or sugarcoating—often when addressing difficult or sensitive subjects.

commitment

A commitment is a dedicated promise, pledge, or firm decision to take a specific action, support a cause, or fulfill an obligation in the future. It represents the focused willingness to allocate your time, energy, and resources to a specific goal, relationship, or task.

Care

Care refers to the provision of what is needed for the health, welfare, maintenance, and protection of someone or something. It encompasses both the active process of looking after others and the state of mental concern, attention, or caution applied to a specific task.
