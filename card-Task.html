Samurai

Samurai were the elite, professional warrior class and hereditary nobility of medieval and early-modern Japan. Emerging in the 8th century, they served as loyal retainers to regional feudal lords (daimyo), eventually rising to political dominance until the abolition of their class in the 1868 Meiji Restoration.
